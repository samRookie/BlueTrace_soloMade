# Phase Boundaries & Scope Protection — Phase 0

## Phase 0 Objective

Phase 0 establishes solely the **technical foundation and monorepo infrastructure** for SIH26019.

## Explicit Out-of-Scope Capabilities

The following capabilities belong to subsequent development phases and are **strictly excluded** from Phase 0:

- **Authentication & Authorization**: No user login, registration, JWT handling, session management, or RBAC.
- **Domain Models & Tables**: No database tables for land parcels, users, policies, or research projects.
- **Repositories & Business Logic**: No business services, domain logic, or persistence repositories.
- **GIS & Spatial Intelligence**: No map viewers, geospatial layers, shapefile processors, or GeoJSON APIs.
- **Blue Carbon & Environmental Analytics**: No carbon credit calculations, coastal sensor models, or ecological models.
- **AI & LLM Services**: No AI agents, policy recommendation engines, chatbots, or vector embeddings.
- **Government System Integrations**: No external land registry APIs, municipal data ingestion, or mock state endpoints.
- **Mock & Fake Data**: No mock dashboards, fake statistics, or dummy user databases.
